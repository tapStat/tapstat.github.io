A Triple Whale alternative for stores that already use GA4

Updated 25 October 2026 · 6 min read

Triple Whale is a strong tool for Shopify brands with large ad budgets: its own pixel, multi-touch attribution, creative analysis and a full mobile app. If what you really want is your revenue, ROAS and profit on your phone, and you already run GA4, you may not need all of that. tapStat is the lighter option.

When Triple Whale is the right choice

To be fair: if you spend heavily on Meta and TikTok, run on Shopify and want pixel-based multi-touch attribution, creative reporting and a team of analysts working in one tool, Triple Whale is built for that, and tapStat isn't trying to replace it.

When tapStat fits better

  • You're not on Shopify. tapStat works with any store that sends ecommerce events to GA4: WooCommerce, Shopware, Magento, BigCommerce, Lightspeed or your own build.
  • You don't want another pixel. tapStat reads the GA4 data you already collect, with read-only access. Nothing to install on your site.
  • You want a phone-first app at a small-store price. Free for one store. Pro is €7 a month or €70 a year.
  • You want answers more than dashboards. Why it changed, alerts that know your normal week, and a morning brief.

What you give up with GA4 data

GA4 misses some orders because of cookie consent and ad blockers, and its attribution is GA4's, not a dedicated pixel's. For most small and mid-sized stores that's fine for daily decisions, especially if you watch trends and keep the gap with your store steady. Read why GA4 revenue doesn't match Shopify to see what's normal.

See your ads and profit in tapStat

Connect Google Ads to GA4 and turn on GA4's cost data import for Meta (our guide shows how). tapStat then shows spend, revenue and ROAS per channel and campaign, merges paid Facebook and Instagram into one Meta Ads channel, and in Pro adds MER, wasted ad spend and profit after product costs, fees, shipping and ads.

Questions

Is there a cheaper alternative to Triple Whale?

If you already use GA4, tapStat shows revenue, ROAS, MER and profit on your phone for free on one store, or €7 a month for Pro. It doesn't do pixel-based multi-touch attribution.

Does tapStat work without Shopify?

Yes. tapStat works with any store platform that sends ecommerce events to Google Analytics 4.

Can I use tapStat and Triple Whale together?

Yes. Some stores use Triple Whale for attribution and tapStat for a quick GA4 view, alerts and widgets on their phone.
