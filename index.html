Tapstat

Your store's Google Analytics, made for your pocket.

Tapstat is an Android app for ecommerce owners. It turns your Google Analytics 4 data into a clear daily view of revenue, orders, products and marketing.

What Tapstat does

  • A dashboard of today's revenue, orders, conversion rate and average order value, compared with last week.
  • Product drill-downs: best sellers, items viewed but not bought, refunds.
  • Marketing channels and campaigns, with spend and return on ad spend when you import costs into GA4.
  • Home screen widgets, a Wear OS tile, and alerts when sales drop or spike.
  • Optional daily or weekly digests and an assistant that answers questions about your store.

How Tapstat uses your Google data

When you sign in with Google, Tapstat asks for read-only access to Google Analytics (analytics.readonly). It uses that access only to list the GA4 properties you can see and to run reports on them, so it can show your store's numbers in the app, in widgets, in alerts and in digests. Tapstat never changes anything in your Google Analytics account, does not sell your data, and does not use it for advertising. Read the privacy policy for the details.
