How tapStat uses your Google data

When you sign in with Google, tapStat asks for read-only access to Google Analytics (analytics.readonly). It uses that access only to list the GA4 properties you can see and to run reports on them, so it can show your store's numbers in the app, in widgets, in alerts and in digests. tapStat never changes anything in your Google Analytics account, does not sell your data, and does not use it for advertising.

Read the privacy policy for the details, including Google's Limited Use requirements.

Questions

Which stores does tapStat work with?

Any online store that sends ecommerce events to Google Analytics 4: Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, Shopware, Lightspeed, BigCommerce or your own build. If you can see purchases in GA4, tapStat can show them.

Do I need to change anything in Google Analytics?

No. tapStat reads the data you already collect. To see ad spend for Meta, TikTok or Pinterest, turn on GA4's cost data import; our guide shows how.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. tapStat is on Android and Wear OS today. iPhone is on the roadmap; vote for it if you want it sooner.

Can I log in on this website?

No. Everything happens in the app. This site is where you learn about tapStat, follow the roadmap and read our guides.

When can I install it?

tapStat is finishing Google's verification and will be on Google Play soon. Ask for early access and you'll hear the day it is live.
