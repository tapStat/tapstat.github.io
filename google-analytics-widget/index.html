A Google Analytics 4 widget for your home screen

Updated 25 October 2026 · 5 min read

The official Google Analytics app doesn't offer a home screen widget, so you have to open the app, pick a property and wait for a report to know anything. tapStat puts today's revenue from your GA4 property right on your Android home screen, your lock screen and your Wear OS watch.

What a store widget should show

Most GA4 widgets show users or sessions. That says little to a store owner. A widget for a store should answer "how are we doing today?" at a glance:

  • Today's revenue so far, gross, the way you think about sales.
  • Pace: ahead of or behind the same weekday last week, up to the same hour.
  • Orders, so one big order doesn't fool you.
  • A tap that opens the full picture, not a blank report.

How to add it

  1. Install tapStat and sign in with Google. tapStat asks for read-only access to Google Analytics and lists the GA4 properties your account can see.
  2. Pick your store's property. The dashboard opens on today's revenue.
  3. Long-press your home screen, tap Widgets and find tapStat. Drag the widget where you want it. It refreshes through the day.

Other ways to get GA4 on your home screen

To be fair to the alternatives:

  • A bookmark to a GA4 report. Free, but it opens a desktop website on a small screen.
  • General GA4 widget apps. A few Android apps, such as Widgets for Google Analytics (GA4), show any GA4 metric as a widget, including realtime users. They're a good fit for content sites. They don't focus on store numbers like pace, orders or ROAS.
  • The Google Analytics app. No widget, but it's free and covers every report in GA4. Many people keep both.

Questions

Does the Google Analytics app have a widget?

No. The official Google Analytics app for Android doesn't offer a home screen widget as of October 2026. You need a third-party app such as tapStat to see GA4 numbers without opening an app.

Is there a Google Analytics widget for iPhone?

tapStat is on Android and Wear OS today. iPhone is on the roadmap.

Does the widget use my real GA4 data?

Yes. tapStat reads your GA4 property through Google's Analytics Data API with read-only access. It never changes anything in your account.

How fresh is the number?

The widget shows what GA4 reports for today. Today's numbers in GA4 usually run a little behind real orders, so treat it as a close reading, not a till receipt.
