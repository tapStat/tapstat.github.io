Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy explains what data the tapStat Android app (package app.betterga), its Wear OS tile and its optional server ("tapStat", "we") access, how it is used, and how you can remove it.

Data we access

  • Your Google account email address, to show which account is signed in and, if you connect the optional server, to identify your account and send email digests.
  • Google Analytics data, read-only (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/analytics.readonly): the list of GA4 accounts and properties you can access, and report data for the property you choose, such as revenue, transactions, items, traffic sources and campaigns.
  • Your settings: the selected property, alert rules, digest preferences and, if you turn on push alerts, a Firebase Cloud Messaging device token.

We do not access any other Google data, your contacts, your location or your files.

How we use it

  • In the app. Reports are requested directly from Google's Analytics Data API and Admin API by your phone and shown in the app, its widgets and the Wear OS tile. Recent results are cached on your device so the app opens quickly. This data stays on your device.
  • Optional server features. If you choose Connect to tapStat server, the app sends us a one-time Google authorization code. Our server (Google Cloud Functions and Firestore, hosted by Google in the EU) stores your email address, a Google refresh token limited to read-only Analytics access, your device tokens, the selected property, alert rules and digest preferences. It uses them only to run Analytics reports on a schedule, check your alert rules, and send you push notifications (through Firebase Cloud Messaging) and email digests (through our email provider, Resend).
  • Optional assistant. When you ask the assistant a question, your question and the Analytics report results needed to answer it are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate the answer. Anthropic processes this data on our behalf and does not use it to train its models.

Limited Use disclosure

tapStat's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically: we use Google user data only to provide and improve the features described above that you can see in the app. We do not sell it, do not use it for advertising, do not use it to train general AI models, and do not transfer it to anyone except the service providers named above as needed to run those features, as required by law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to you. No person at tapStat reads your data unless you ask us to for support, it is needed for security or to comply with the law.

Sharing

We share data only with the service providers needed to run the features you use: Google (Cloud hosting, Firestore, Firebase Cloud Messaging), Resend (email digests) and Anthropic (assistant answers). We do not sell personal data.

Retention and deletion

  • Data cached on your phone is deleted when you sign out or uninstall the app.
  • Server data is kept while your account is connected. Settings › Disconnect in the app revokes our Google token and deletes your server record immediately.
  • You can also remove tapStat's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To ask us to delete your data, email us at the address below. We reply within 30 days.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Server data is encrypted at rest by Google Cloud, and our database denies all direct client access. Refresh tokens are only readable by our server code.

This website

This website (tapstat.app) has no accounts and no login; everything happens in the app. To understand how visitors find and use the site, it uses Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager, which set cookies and collect usage data such as pages viewed, device type, approximate location and the website that sent you; see how Google uses this data. Website data is never combined with the Analytics data you view in the app. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log your IP address for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. Roadmap votes and ideas are collected on our public board at Featurebase (tapstat.featurebase.app), which has its own privacy policy. If you email us, for example to ask for early access, we use your email address only to reply and to tell you about tapStat, and we delete it on request.

Children

tapStat is a business tool and is not directed at children under 16.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to its processing. Email us and we will help. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
