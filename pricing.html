Pricing questions

How does the free trial work?

Pro and Team start with 14 days free. You are not charged during the trial, and you can cancel any time in Google Play.

Is the price in euros or dollars?

Pro is 7 a month or 70 a year in euros or US dollars. Google Play shows the price in your local currency, including tax where it applies.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Subscriptions run through Google Play, where you can cancel in two taps. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

Why isn't tapStat free, like Google's app?

Because we work only for store owners. No ads, no selling data, and every feature is designed around one question: how is my store doing and why. Your subscription pays for the servers, the AI assistant and the next feature on the roadmap.

Does the free plan expire?

No. Free stays free for one store, with today, yesterday and the last 7 days.

Do you offer a discount for agencies?

If you manage many clients' stores, email us. Agency pricing is on the roadmap.
