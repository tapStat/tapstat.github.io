Team questions

Do team members need access to our Google Analytics?

Yes. tapStat only reads data each person can already see in GA4. Give teammates Viewer access to the property in Google Analytics, and they'll see it in tapStat.

We have 2 people. Should we take Team?

Two Pro subscriptions cost €14 a month, so Pro is cheaper for two. Team pays off from the third person.

We have more than 5 people.

Email us. Larger teams and agency plans are on the roadmap, and we'd like to hear what you need.

When can we start?

The Team plan opens together with tapStat's release on Google Play. Reserve a spot and we'll set your team up first.
