Watch every client store from one screen

Updated 25 October 2026 · 5 min read

When you look after ten client stores, "how are we doing?" arrives on a Saturday morning by WhatsApp. tapStat puts every client's GA4 property on one screen, sorted so the store that needs attention is on top, and answers the follow-up question too: why.

What agencies get stuck on today

  • Switching properties. The Google Analytics app shows one property at a time. Checking ten clients means ten switches and ten reports.
  • Dashboards built for desktops. Looker Studio reports are great on a monitor and painful on a phone.
  • Explaining a dip. The client wants to know why, not just what. That usually means opening a laptop.

Access stays where it is

tapStat signs in with each person's own Google account and reads only the GA4 properties that account can already see, with read-only access. You don't share logins or hand over client data to a new tool. If a client removes your access in Google Analytics, the store disappears from tapStat too.

Questions

Can I see multiple GA4 properties in one app?

Yes. tapStat lists every GA4 property your Google account can access. Pro adds an all-stores screen and widget that show them side by side.

Do my clients need to install anything?

No. You need Viewer access to their GA4 property, which most agencies already have. Clients can receive email digests without installing the app.

Does tapStat change anything in client accounts?

No. tapStat only asks for read-only Google Analytics access and can't change settings, tags or data.
