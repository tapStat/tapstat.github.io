Sales alerts that don't cry wolf

Updated 25 October 2026 · 5 min read

An alert is only useful if you trust it. Most store alerts compare today with yesterday, so every quiet Sunday and every half-processed GA4 day fires a warning, and after a week you mute them. tapStat's alerts know your normal week and wait for GA4 to settle.

What GA4 offers on its own

GA4 has custom insights: rules such as "revenue fell by more than 20%" that send an email or show in the Insights panel. They work, with limits:

  • They arrive by email, not as a push notification on your phone.
  • A fixed percentage against the previous day ignores weekday patterns.
  • Conversion rate alerts on the newest day fire while GA4 is still attributing late sessions, which makes recent conversion rates look low. See why GA4 conversion rate dips.

How tapStat alerts work

  1. Each day is compared with its own weekday. Revenue on a Tuesday is compared with the median of the last four Tuesdays. One unusual week doesn't throw the baseline off.
  2. Fast metrics check yesterday. Revenue, orders, order value and refunds settle quickly, so they're checked the next morning.
  3. Conversion rate checks the newest settled day. It waits until GA4 has finished attributing late sessions, and tells you so up front.
  4. Tracking health is watched separately. If purchase data stops arriving, you get a different alert, because it's a different problem.

When an alert fires

Tap it and tapStat opens on the number with Why it changed: traffic, conversion rate or order value, and the channel, device or product behind it. You go from "something is wrong" to "the Meta campaign stopped" in two taps.

Questions

Can Google Analytics send alerts when sales drop?

GA4's custom insights can email you when a metric changes by a set amount. They don't send push notifications and don't account for weekday patterns.

Why do I get false conversion rate alerts?

GA4 keeps revising the last 24 to 48 hours while late traffic sources are attributed, which makes recent conversion rates look too low. Alerts on the newest day fire falsely. tapStat checks conversion rate on a settled day.

Do alerts work for more than one store?

Alerts follow the store you have selected in the app. To keep an eye on several stores, the all-stores screen in Pro shows them side by side, with the one that needs attention on top.
