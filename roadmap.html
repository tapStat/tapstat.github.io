How we decide

  1. You vote and suggest. Every vote and idea is read by the person building tapStat.
  2. We weigh it against the mission. tapStat answers one question for store owners: how is my store doing, and why? Ideas that make that answer faster or clearer go first.
  3. We ship small and often, and write down every change in the changelog.
